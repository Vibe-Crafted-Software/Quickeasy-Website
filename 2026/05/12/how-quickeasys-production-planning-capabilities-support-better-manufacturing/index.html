How QuickEasy’s Production Planning Capabilities Support Better Manufacturing

12 May 2026

Manufacturing is hard to control when your production planning software is actually a spreadsheet, your stock figures live somewhere else, your job cards are written by hand, and your costs only become clear once the job is finished.

QuickEasy’s production planning capabilities help manufacturers plan, schedule, track and cost production from one connected system. Manage work orders, job cards, routings, Gantt charts, Kanban boards, materials, timekeeping, operator tracking, work centre loading, non-conformance reporting, inventory, costing, dashboards and reports inside the same manufacturing ERP environment. QuickEasy gives your team a clearer view of production while work is happening, not only once the month is over.

Jump ahead: Request a demo

Key takeaways

  • QuickEasy BOS ERP improves production planning by connecting the factory floor with inventory, purchasing, costing, finance and reporting.
  • Our production planning capabilities support work orders, job cards, routing forms, multiple routings, Kanban boards, Gantt charts, material requisitions, work centre loading, operator assignments, timekeeping, job tracking, NCR, dashboards and interactive reports.
  • QuickEasy is suitable for different manufacturing environments: printing and packaging, plastics, engineering, assembly, jobbing shops, make-to-order production, make-to-stock production, batch manufacturing and multi-stage production.
  • The real benefits are clarity and control. See what is planned, what is available, what has changed, what is delayed, what is complete and what it is costing the business.

What does QuickEasy’s production planning software do?

QuickEasy’s production planning functionality helps you turn demand into controlled production work. That demand may come from a customer order, a forecast, a replenishment requirement, a repeat production run, or an internal stock requirement. From there, QuickEasy helps you plan what needs to be made, check what materials are required, schedule the work, assign tasks, track progress, manage changes, and report on performance.

Because production planning sits inside QuickEasy BOS ERP, it does not operate in isolation. It connects to inventory management, purchasing, sales, CRM, accounting, costing and reporting. QuickEasy helps you see the links sooner.

Production Planning built for real manufacturing

Production rarely follows the neat version of the plan. Materials arrive late. Machines go down. Jobs take longer than expected. Operators become unavailable. Customers change dates. Urgent work jumps the queue. A work centre becomes overloaded while another has capacity.

QuickEasy supports the real way manufacturing works by giving your team flexible planning functionality, multiple production views, live job tracking, material visibility and connected reporting. This is useful whether you manufacture standard products, custom jobs, printed materials, components, assemblies, packaging, engineered items, batches, sub-assemblies or finished goods.

You do not need every department to work from the same screen. You need every department to work from the same information.

Plan production your way

Different manufacturers need different production documents and workflows. A printing business may need job cards, routings and JDF/Switch integration. An engineering business may need work orders, labour tracking and cost centre visibility. A packaging manufacturer may need material planning, multi-stage production and job costing. A make-to-order business may need every job linked closely to a customer requirement. QuickEasy supports production planning in a way that can fit your operation.

Work orders

Work orders help your team understand what needs to be produced, in what quantity, and by when. In a busy factory, this matters because verbal instructions and scattered notes create risk. A work order gives production a structured record of the job. It helps your team see the product, quantity, due date, requirements and production status more clearly. For management, work orders also create better visibility. You can see what work is open, what has started, what is complete and what needs attention.

Job cards

Job cards give the factory floor the practical instructions needed to complete the job. A job card may include the customer, product, quantity, production steps, materials, routing, notes, dates and job-specific requirements. In a manual environment, those details often sit on paper, in emails, or with the person who “knows the job”.

That becomes risky as the business grows. With QuickEasy, job cards form part of your ERP record. They can connect to materials, labour, timekeeping, cost tracking, job status and reporting. That means the job card is not just a production instruction. It becomes part of your operational and financial control.

Routing forms

Routing forms help your team define the production path a job must follow. This is important when work moves through different stages, machines, departments, work centres or cost centres. For example, a job may need cutting, printing, finishing, assembly, inspection and despatch. Each step affects timing, labour, material usage and cost. Routing forms help make that path visible, so teams know what needs to happen next and where the job should move.

Multiple routings

The same product or job may not always follow the same route. A small run may use one workflow. A larger run may need a different machine. A custom customer requirement may add another production step. A material shortage may require a different process. A finishing requirement may change the route. QuickEasy supports multiple routings, giving your production team more flexibility when planning work.

This helps you avoid forcing every job through a rigid process when your actual production environment needs more room to move.

JDF and Switch integration for printing businesses

Printing businesses often need production planning to connect with print-specific workflows. QuickEasy supports JDF and Switch integration for printing environments, helping businesses manage job information and workflow requirements more smoothly. For printers, this can help reduce manual handovers between estimating, prepress, production and workflow automation. It also gives production teams clearer job information inside the wider ERP process.

View production work from different angles

A production manager, buyer, operator, supervisor, finance manager and business owner do not all need the same view. The production manager may need scheduling visibility. The buyer needs to see material demand. The supervisor needs to know what is late. Operators need to know what to work on next. Finance needs to understand costs. Management needs a clear view of performance.

QuickEasy gives your team multiple interactive production views, so each role can work with the information that matters most.

Kanban boards

Kanban boards help your team see work moving through production stages. This is useful when you need a quick visual view of what is waiting, active, blocked, delayed or complete. Instead of searching through long job lists, supervisors can see where work is sitting and what needs attention. In real-world production, this helps with daily planning. Your team can quickly see where the factory is moving and where it is getting stuck.

Jobs view

The jobs view helps your team manage production jobs in a focused way. You can see job details, statuses, dates, quantities and progress. This is useful when your team is managing multiple open jobs and needs to understand which ones are active, late, waiting for materials or ready for the next step. For manufacturers with many jobs moving at once, this view helps reduce the need for constant manual follow-ups.

Materials view

Production cannot move if the right materials are not available. The materials view helps your team check what is needed and whether it is available before work starts. This helps production, inventory and purchasing work together more effectively. In practice, this can prevent a common factory problem: the job is scheduled, the operator is ready, the machine is available, but one material is missing. When material requirements are visible earlier, your team has more time to act.

Timekeeping view

Labour is one of the biggest factors in production cost and efficiency. The timekeeping view helps you see how much time is being spent on production tasks. This helps your team compare planned time against actual time and understand where jobs are taking longer than expected. This is useful for quoting, costing, scheduling and performance improvement. If a certain process regularly takes longer than planned, you can investigate the cause and adjust your planning.

Board view

The board view helps your team visualise production across work centres, tasks or departments. This is useful for daily production management because it shows where work is sitting and where pressure is building. A supervisor can see whether one area is overloaded while another has capacity. That makes it easier to balance work, shift priorities or communicate delays before they become bigger issues.

Gantt charts

Gantt charts help your team see production timelines, sequencing and dependencies. This is valuable when jobs have multiple stages that must happen in order. If one task moves, the rest of the plan may need to move too. A Gantt view helps production managers see how timing, dependencies and deadlines connect. For manufacturers with tight delivery dates, this is one of the most practical ways to manage time risk.

Production planning functionality to manage real production constraints

A production plan is only useful if it reflects what your factory can realistically do. QuickEasy helps you plan around real constraints such as available capacity, material readiness, operator availability, job dependencies, required dates and production bottlenecks.

Work centre loading

Work centre loading helps you see how much work is assigned to each machine, department, workstation or cost centre. This is important because a plan can look fine until one area becomes overloaded. For example, cutting may be fully booked, while assembly has capacity. If you cannot see that early, the delay only becomes obvious when jobs start missing deadlines. With work centre loading, your production manager can make better scheduling decisions before the pressure becomes a customer problem.

Material requisition progress

Material requisitions help control the movement of materials into production. QuickEasy helps your team see whether materials have been requested, issued, delayed or still need attention. This helps reduce informal stock movement and improves control between inventory and production. In the real world, this protects both production flow and stock accuracy. Your team can see whether the materials needed for a job are actually ready, not just assumed to be available.

Estimated versus actual time

Estimated time helps you plan. Actual time shows what happened. QuickEasy helps you compare the two, so you can understand where production is running according to plan and where it is not. If a task regularly takes longer than expected, the issue may be machine setup, operator training, incorrect estimates, material quality, rework or workflow design. Without the comparison, the same problem can keep affecting jobs quietly. This visibility helps you improve future planning and quoting.

Estimated versus actual quantities

Production quantity matters because differences affect stock, costing and margin. QuickEasy helps you compare what you expected to produce with what was actually produced. This can highlight wastage, spoilage, short runs, overproduction, rework, quality issues or material problems. For manufacturers, this is important because quantity differences do not only affect the job. They also affect inventory values, customer commitments and profitability.

Required dates and key job dates

Manufacturing teams need to plan around dates. Required dates, production start dates, due dates, customer delivery dates and key job dates all affect scheduling. QuickEasy helps your team plan and filter work using important dates, so production can focus on the right jobs at the right time. This is especially useful when priorities change and your team needs to decide what must move first.

Production bottlenecks

A bottleneck is the point where work slows down the rest of the operation. It may be a machine, work centre, approval step, material shortage, operator constraint or quality issue. QuickEasy gives your team clearer visibility of late jobs, busy work centres, work centre loading, material requisition progress and job dependencies. This helps management see where pressure is building while there is still time to act.

Operator availability

Production depends on people as much as materials and machines. QuickEasy helps you consider operator assignments and availability when planning work. This helps reduce unrealistic scheduling where jobs are planned without understanding whether the right people are available to do the work. It also helps supervisors manage labour more effectively across shifts, work centres and active jobs.

Job dependencies

Some production tasks cannot start until another task is complete. QuickEasy supports dependency-based planning, helping your team manage the relationship between tasks. If one task is delayed, the impact can be reflected in the wider plan. This gives you a more realistic schedule and reduces the risk of teams working from outdated information.

Track progress while work is happening

Planning is one part of manufacturing control. Tracking is where your team sees whether the plan is holding. QuickEasy helps you monitor production activity while work is in progress. That gives management a clearer view of what is moving, what is stuck, what is late and what needs attention.

Task progress by quantity

Task progress by quantity helps you see how much work has been completed. This is useful when progress is measured in units, batches, components, metres, kilograms, litres or finished items. Your team can see how far the job has moved and how much still needs to be completed. For production managers, this makes progress more visible than a simple “in progress” status.

Task progress by hours

Task progress by hours helps you see how much labour time has been used. This is important for production control and job costing. If a job is using more hours than expected, your team can investigate before the full margin has already been lost. It also helps with future planning because you can compare estimated labour time with actual labour time.

Operator assignments

Operator assignments help your team see who is responsible for each task. This supports accountability, workload planning and better follow-up. It also helps management understand how labour is being used across jobs and work centres. In a busy factory, this reduces confusion because teams can see who is assigned and what each person is working on.

Colour-coded routing statuses

Colour-coded routing statuses make production easier to read quickly. Instead of opening each job to understand what is happening, your team can identify progress, delays or required action at a glance. This is useful for supervisors who need to manage many tasks at once. Simple visibility can make a big difference when production is under pressure.

Late jobs

Late jobs need to be visible early. QuickEasy helps your team identify delayed jobs, so production managers can decide what action to take. That may mean rescheduling, reallocating work, checking materials, escalating a blockage or communicating with sales. The earlier a late job is visible, the more options your team has.

Busy work centres

Busy work centres can slow the whole factory down. QuickEasy helps you see where capacity is under pressure. This makes it easier to balance workload, adjust priorities or plan around constraints. For example, if finishing is overloaded but another area is ready, your team can make more informed decisions instead of discovering the issue only when the job stops moving.

Time to start

Time to start helps your team see when a task is due to begin. This supports better daily planning because supervisors can see what is coming next and whether the required materials, people and work centres are ready. It helps reduce idle time caused by unclear sequencing or missing preparation.

Job status changes

Job status changes help keep the production record current. As work starts, pauses, moves, completes or changes, QuickEasy helps reflect those updates in the system. This gives production, sales, customer service, finance and management a clearer view of what is happening. That reduces the need for everyone to ask production for updates manually.

Adjust plans when priorities change

Manufacturing plans change. The issue is not whether they change. The issue is how quickly your team can respond without creating confusion. QuickEasy gives your team tools to adjust production planning when materials are delayed, customer dates move, machines go down, or urgent work needs to be prioritised.

Reset planning from now

Sometimes the original production schedule no longer reflects reality. Reset planning from now helps your team update the plan from the current point rather than manually rebuilding the schedule. This is useful when the day has already changed and production needs a realistic plan from this moment forward. It gives your team a cleaner way to recover when the plan shifts.

Dependency-based task adjustments

When one production task changes, related tasks may need to change too. QuickEasy supports dependency-based task adjustments, helping your team manage the knock-on effect of delays or changes. This helps prevent later tasks from appearing on track when earlier tasks have already moved. That gives you a more accurate production schedule.

Batching orders by start date and status

Batching orders by start date and status helps your team group work more effectively. This can support better scheduling, reduce unnecessary changeovers and help supervisors manage work with similar timing or production status. For manufacturers handling many jobs, this makes the production plan easier to organise and act on.

Scheduling by required date

Scheduling by required date helps your team prioritise work based on when it is needed. This is useful when you need to protect customer commitments, manage urgent jobs or decide which work should move first. It also helps avoid a simple first-in, first-out approach when the business needs a more practical production priority.

Timeline and dependency visualisation through Gantt diagrams

Gantt diagrams help your team see production timing and dependencies in one view. This makes it easier to understand how each task affects the wider schedule. If a task moves, your team can see what else may be affected. For production managers, this gives better control over deadlines, sequencing and workload.

Connect production planning to the wider business

The strongest manufacturing ERP does more than plan production. It connects production to the rest of the business, so your team can see the operational and financial impact of what is happening on the floor.

QuickEasy BOS ERP connects production planning with timekeeping, operator tracking, inventory management, material requisitions, NCR, cost tracking, dashboards and interactive reports.

Timekeeping

Timekeeping connects labour activity to production work. This helps you understand how long jobs take, how labour is being used and whether actual time matches planned time. It also gives finance and management better information for job costing and margin analysis. For manufacturers, timekeeping is not only an HR function. It is part of production control.

Operator tracking

Operator tracking helps you see who worked on what, when they worked on it, and how that work moved through production. This supports accountability, training, workload planning and performance visibility. It also helps you understand whether production pressure is linked to capacity, operator availability, process design or job complexity.

Inventory management

Production planning depends on accurate inventory. QuickEasy connects production with inventory management, helping your team see raw materials, work in progress, finished goods, stock movements and material availability. This helps reduce stockouts, overbuying, emergency purchasing and production delays caused by missing materials.It also helps protect cash flow. Too much stock ties up money. Too little stock delays production.

Material requisitions

Material requisitions help control what moves from stock into production. When material requisitions are connected to production planning, your team can see what has been requested, what has been issued and what is still outstanding. This reduces informal stock movement and helps keep your inventory records more accurate.

Non-conformance reporting

Non-conformance reporting (NCR) helps your team manage quality issues. When NCR is connected to production, you can link quality problems to the relevant job, material, process, operator or cost impact. This supports better investigation and corrective action. It also helps management understand the true cost of rework, waste and quality problems.

Cost tracking

Cost tracking helps you understand whether jobs are performing as expected. QuickEasy helps connect production activity with material usage, labour time, quantities and costs. This allows your team to compare estimated costs with actual costs. That matters because a job can look profitable when quoted but lose margin through extra labour, material waste, downtime, rework or incorrect estimates. Better cost tracking helps you quote better, plan better and protect margin.

Dashboards

Dashboards give management a clearer view of production performance. Instead of waiting for manual summaries, your team can see important production information more quickly. This may include open jobs, late jobs, busy work centres, material shortages, work in progress, stock usage, costs, bottlenecks and production performance. This helps you make decisions earlier.

Interactive reports

Interactive reports help your team analyse production information in more detail. You can filter, sort and review data to understand what is happening across jobs, materials, labour, costs and performance. This supports better decisions because you are working from production data, not assumptions.

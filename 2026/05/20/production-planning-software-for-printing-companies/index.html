Why print production planning is different

Print production software is not the same as standard manufacturing ERP. In many manufacturing businesses, production is based on fixed bills of materials. The same product is made repeatedly using the same parts, process and production sequence.

Printing companies work differently. A single job may need a specific paper stock, trim size, ink coverage, plate usage, make-ready time, print method, finishing process and delivery date. The next job may need a completely different setup.

Why QuickEasy BOS is the best ERP for printing companies

QuickEasy BOS is built to support the way printing companies operate. We’ve done this for more than 20 years – QuickEasy supports the way print work actually moves through a business. Print companies need to estimate quickly and accurately, manage custom jobs, check material availability, route work through different production steps, track WIP, monitor machine and operator activity, control costs and invoice correctly.

Accurate print estimating before production starts

In printing, production control starts at the estimate. If your estimate is wrong, your margin is already under pressure before the job reaches the floor.

QuickEasy BOS generates estimates that take wastage, ink consumption, machine speed and shop-floor overheads into account. It can also show you how to lay out a job to reduce wastage and calculate the cost of unavoidable wastage.

That matters because print jobs are often custom. You may estimate a brochure, carton, label, booklet, flyer, sign, POS item or packaging job. Each job may use different materials, quantities, machines, finishing steps and delivery expectations. Accurate estimating helps you price the job properly before production starts.

From quote to job card

Once a quote becomes an order, your team needs clear production instructions. QuickEasy BOS supports customisable production documents and workflows, including:

  • Work orders
  • Job cards
  • Routing forms
  • Multiple routings
  • JDF and Switch integration for printing businesses

This is important for printing companies because not every job follows the same route. One job may move through pre-press, digital print, trimming and dispatch. Another may need litho print, lamination, die cutting, folding and binding. Another may need signage production, vinyl, mounting, finishing and delivery. Multiple routings help you manage different production paths without forcing every print job into the same process.

Better control across pre-press, print and finishing

Print production has many moving parts. A job may be waiting for artwork approval. Another may be ready for plates. Another may be on the press. Another may be waiting for lamination, guillotine cutting, folding, binding, die cutting or dispatch. When these steps are managed manually, updates are easy to miss.

QuickEasy BOS helps your team plan and track production using different planning views, including Kanban boards, jobs view, materials view, timekeeping view, board view and Gantt charts. This means different people can see production in the way that helps them do their job. Your production manager can look at workload and bottlenecks. Your stock controller can check material requirements. Your operators can see assigned work. Your customer-facing team can check job progress.

WIP tracking from start to finish

Work in progress is one of the biggest pressure points in a printing company. If WIP is only visible on a whiteboard, paper job card or production manager’s desk, your business becomes dependent on people chasing updates.

QuickEasy BOS helps track WIP from start to finish. Your team can see what is moving, what is stuck, what is late, what has changed status and what needs attention. That makes a practical difference. If a customer asks for an update, your team should not need to walk through the factory or phone three people. They should be able to see the latest job status in the system.

Machine loading and work centre control

In printing, machine time matters. You need to know what is booked on each press, cutter, laminator, binding line or finishing station. You also need to know where pressure is building before it becomes a missed deadline.

QuickEasy BOS helps you plan around production constraints, including:

  • Work centre loading
  • Material requisition progress
  • Estimated versus actual time
  • Estimated versus actual quantities
  • Required dates and key job dates
  • Production bottlenecks
  • Operator availability
  • Job dependencies

Operator tracking and timekeeping

QuickEasy BOS connects production planning with timekeeping and operator tracking. It helps you track progress by quantity and hours, operator assignments, routing statuses, late jobs, busy work centres, time to start and job status changes. This gives you a better view of what is happening on the shop floor while work is in progress. It also helps you build a stronger record of actual production time, which can support better costing and future estimating.

Stock control for print materials

Printing companies need accurate stock control. If paper, board, vinyl, ink, plates, packaging materials or finishing consumables are not available, production can stop. If stock is over-ordered, cash is tied up. If stock has been used but not recorded, your production plan becomes unreliable.

QuickEasy BOS connects production planning with inventory management and material requisitions.

This helps your team check stock availability, plan purchasing requirements, issue materials to production, track stock usage and keep production moving with fewer surprises. For printing companies, that link between stock and production is essential. A job cannot run smoothly if the materials are missing, allocated elsewhere or incorrectly recorded.

Estimated costs versus actual costs

Printing margins can disappear quietly. A job may look profitable when quoted, but the actual result can change because of extra waste, longer machine time, rework, overtime, material changes or delays.

Production planning connected to the rest of your printing business

A print job affects more than production. It affects sales, estimating, stock, purchasing, customer service, finance, invoicing and management reporting.

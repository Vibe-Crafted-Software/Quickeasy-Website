Cloud-Based ERP

17 August 2026

If you already know your business needs ERP, there is another important decision to make. Where do you want that ERP to live? You can maintain the server environment yourself, take responsibility for backups, hardware and the technical infrastructure behind it, or you can opt for the ease and security of cloud-based ERP.

QuickEasy BOS gives you a complete cloud-based ERP without requiring you to run the core ERP server environment from your own premises. You still get the depth of QuickEasy ERP for SMEs across finance, distribution, manufacturing, sales and wider business operations. The difference is that QuickEasy can host the ERP environment for you, giving authorised users remote access without your business having to maintain the main BOS server on site. QuickEasy also supports locally hosted deployments where that is the better fit.

For many growing operational businesses, this is why QuickEasy BOS is such a strong cloud-based ERP choice. You get the power of a complete ERP, while reducing how much of the technology underneath it you have to worry about yourself.

Ready for the best cloud-based ERP? Let’s talk.

Key takeaways

  • QuickEasy BOS is a complete cloud-based ERP for businesses that want their core ERP environment hosted rather than maintained entirely on their own premises.
  • QuickEasy supports both cloud and local hosting.
  • For dispersed teams, or growing teams, cloud-based ERP systems are ideal.
  • Cloud-hosted QuickEasy can make ERP access easier for businesses operating across offices, factories, warehouses and other locations.
  • For QuickEasy-managed AWS hosting, we offer daily backups, securities, and recovery provisions.
  • Cloud hosting is not automatically right for every company. Connectivity, IT requirements, control and business continuity should all form part of the decision.

What is cloud-based ERP?

Cloud-based ERP is simply business management software that runs online, rather than being installed and hosted on a server at your own premises. With QuickEasy BOS cloud-based ERP, your business gets access to QuickEasy’s wider ERP system, while the main BOS system is hosted securely in the cloud.

If you host your ERP on your own server, your business is responsible for that server. You need to maintain it, keep it secure, back it up, deal with problems, and eventually replace it.

With QuickEasy BOS in the cloud, much more of that responsibility can sit with QuickEasy instead.

That means:

  • Less hardware for your business to buy and maintain
  • Less reliance on an on-site server
  • Less technical infrastructure for your team to worry about.

You still get the single system you need to run your entire business, but without having to manage as much of the technology that keeps them running.

Why run QuickEasy BOS in the cloud?

Easier access when your business operates in more than one place

Very few operational businesses now exist neatly inside one building. You may have an office and a factory. Stock may sit at another warehouse. Your financial manager may occasionally work from home. Management may travel between locations.

This is one of the clearest advantages of running QuickEasy in a hosted environment.

Your ERP does not have to be physically tied to one office server for authorised users to reach the hosted system. For a business with several locations, this can be far simpler than trying to make every branch depend on infrastructure sitting at head office.

Cloud-based does not necessarily mean browser-based

This is one of the areas where ERP terminology becomes confusing. A system can be cloud-hosted without running inside Chrome, Edge or another web browser.

They are not interchangeable terms. This matters when you compare ERP products.

Don’t assume a product is more or less capable simply because it opens in a browser. Look at how the software performs, how users access it, how the hosting is managed, and whether the architecture suits the work your business actually does.

We deliver a native BOS environment through cloud infrastructure and virtualisation. This approach allows QuickEasy to retain the performance and functionality required by more complex ERP workloads.

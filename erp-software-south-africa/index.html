ERP software South Africa

ERP Software for South African Businesses

One system for quoting, production, stock, purchasing and accounting — built in South Africa, priced in rand, supported by the people who wrote it.

QuickEasy Software has been building business systems for South African companies since 1998. BOS is a complete, cloud-based ERP used by printers, packaging and signage companies, manufacturers and engineering firms across the country.

Why local matters

An ERP that already speaks South African.

Global platforms make you configure your way to compliance. BOS ships with it.

VAT and SARS-ready reporting

South African VAT handling and financial reporting are part of the core ledger — not a localisation pack bought on top.

Priced and invoiced in rand

Licensing is quoted in ZAR, so your software cost does not move every time the exchange rate does.

Support in your time zone

Implementation and support come from the same company that writes the software, working the hours you work.

From the quote to the ledger

Everything the business runs on, in one place.

BOS covers the operational chain most South African SMEs currently spread across three or four disconnected tools.

Estimating and quoting

Accurate, configurable quotes from templates or from scratch, with real costing logic behind every line.

Production and scheduling

Turn won quotes into job cards, load machines, and track work in progress on the floor in real time.

Stock and procurement

Multi-warehouse inventory, reorder points and purchase orders driven by what production actually needs.

Job costing

Every hour, material and transaction lands against the job, so you see the true margin — not an estimate of it.

Full accounting

Double-entry accounting, VAT and financial statements built in, sharing one set of numbers with operations.

Reporting and dashboards

Drill-down reporting across the whole business, because every module writes to the same database.

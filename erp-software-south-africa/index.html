The problem

The business outgrew the way it runs.

Not dramatically. One spreadsheet at a time, until nobody can answer a simple question without opening four things.

The numbers disagree

Stock says one thing, accounts another, and the person who reconciles them is the only one who knows which to believe.

Everything is re-keyed

The same order typed into a quote, a job card, a purchase order and an invoice, because none of those systems talk.

Reporting is an archaeology project

Month end takes a week, and by the time the numbers arrive they describe a situation that has already changed.

The big ERPs are priced for someone else

Global suites arrive with licence tiers, implementation partners and a capital budget that does not fit a South African mid-sized business.

The benefit

A system that fits the business and the budget.

One version of the truth

Finance, stock, production and sales read the same record, so the reconciliation meeting stops being necessary.

Priced in rands, published

Month-to-month, per user, no capital outlay and no lock-in. The pricing is on the website rather than behind a discovery call.

Support in your timezone

A South African helpdesk by email, phone and remote access — not a ticket queue in another hemisphere.

The solution

What QuickEasy BOS covers.

Every module is in every subscription. There is no edition that unlocks the function you turn out to need.

  • Full-house accounting — IFRS and GAAP compliant, multi-entity, multi-currency, multi-location.
  • Stock and inventory — multiple warehouses and bins, batch and serial tracking, weighted-average costing.
  • Estimating and quoting — multi-quantity, multi-currency, with a fixed module for print, packaging and signage.
  • Production — bills of materials, routing, MRP, capacity planning and shop-floor data collection.
  • Procurement — multiple suppliers per item, automatic landing costs, EDI built in.
  • CRM and customer service — unlimited customer records, full interaction history, ticketing by priority and severity.
  • Analytics — dashboards, built-in reports, pivot tables, drill-down and comparative views.

Before you commit.

  • "ERP means a capital project." Not here. Month-to-month, per user, no initial outlay, no lock-in. The prices are published.

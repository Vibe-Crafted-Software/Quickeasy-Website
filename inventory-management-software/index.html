Inventory management software

The quantity on screen is the quantity you have.

Real-time stock across every location, tied directly to purchasing, production and the ledger — so its value is already in the books rather than waiting for a month-end reconciliation.

Book a demo See pricing

The problem

Most stock problems are integration problems.

When inventory lives in its own app, it tells you what you had at the last sync. Four things go wrong, and all of them cost cash.

You buy what you already have

Purchasing cannot see what production has committed, so stock gets ordered twice and the cash sits on a shelf until someone finds it.

Valuations drift from the ledger

A separate stock system means a month-end reconciliation just to learn what your inventory is worth — and an argument about which number is right.

Shortages surface too late

Without demand-driven replenishment you find the missing material on the day the job was scheduled to run.

Nobody trusts the count

Once the number on screen has been wrong a few times, people stop looking and start walking to the shelf. At that point the system is costing you time rather than saving it.

The benefit

Stock you can plan against, value and trace.

One number, everywhere

Sales, production, purchasing and accounts read the same live figure, so the reconciliation conversation stops happening.

Cash released from the shelf

Replenishment driven by real demand rather than by habit, with the levels set by you and visible.

Traceable when it matters

Batch and serial tracking back to raw material, ready for the customer audit rather than reconstructed for it.

The solution

Inventory as part of the system, not bolted onto it.

Levels update off daily transactions, because the transactions and the stock records are the same database.

  • Multiple locations, warehouses and bins — including customer and supplier sites, with transfers between them.
  • Multiple units of measure — buy by the tonne, hold by the sheet, consume by the square metre, converted every time.
  • Landing costs allocated automatically to inventory items, so imported stock is valued on what it actually cost to land.
  • Batch and serial number tracking for traceability, warranty and compliance.
  • MRP and replenishment that net demand against free stock and open purchase orders before suggesting a purchase.
  • Weighted-average costing updated automatically, keeping valuation and the ledger in step.
  • Stock checks with approval workflows, bin management and an on-the-fly adjustment mode from the items dashboard.
  • Work in progress tracked as WIP, so material issued to jobs is not counted as available.

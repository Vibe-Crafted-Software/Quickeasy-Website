Why BOS Professional

Everything you need to run print, signage & packaging

Quick installation. Easy to use. Friendly support.

QuickEasy BOS can be configured to match any transaction flow, no matter how simple or complicated it is.

  • Installation is quick and easy.
  • Our software can run on your local network or from a cloud server.
  • Getting started is easy with no initial capital outlay required.
  • Our cost-effective subscription model makes it as affordable to a single-user home business as it is to a medium or large company.
  • Great helpdesk support means you always enjoy great service.
A central database for every client and each interaction.
  • Store unlimited customer information.
  • Keep leads and customer data in one central source.
  • Store all the customer information that is important to you — you decide what you want to see.
  • Whether your reps come or go, you keep the data.
  • Follow-up reminders mean hot leads never grow cold.
  • Track and monitor sales rep activity.
  • Send personalised mailshots to targeted customer groups.
Don't estimate. Be precise.
  • BOS Professional can generate incredibly accurate quotes that take wastage, ink consumption, machine speed and shop floor overheads into account. It will even show you how to lay out a job to reduce wastage and will calculate the exact cost of unavoidable wastage to the penny.
  • So even inexperienced estimators can create accurate quotes — no matter how bespoke the job is.
Compare estimates with actual costs and profit.

See exactly where your expenditure is going — and where you're generating profit. You can also track the return on investment of all your sales activities. With this knowledge, you can pinpoint improvement opportunities right across your business, and generate more profit from every job.

Clarity and control over your stock and inventory.
  • Enjoy a constant helicopter view of your stock and inventory.
  • Sales orders and purchase orders are on the same workflow.
  • Minimise errors and loss.
  • Replenishment levels are set by you.
  • Inventory levels are automatically updated by daily transactions.
  • No more forgotten orders or over/under ordering.
  • Trace products back to production batch, including raw materials.
  • Only items in stock are shown as inventory items — no more bloated inventory lists.
  • Multiple supplier prices, production methods and warehouse locations.
  • Manage your inventory on one system with ease.
Insight into production and WIP.
  • No more whiteboards, paper rosters or sticky note reminders.
  • No more working in silos. Reduce owner-dependency. Improve communication.
  • Planning board keeps everyone in the loop.
  • Work in progress (WIP) tracked from start to finish.
  • Customers can always have the latest status on their job, regardless of who they speak to.
  • Always know what your team is doing.
  • Time tracker measures team productivity and machine productivity.
  • Improve estimating accuracy based on historical time tracking data.
  • Optimise shifts and staff utilisation. Improve efficiency across all stations.
  • No more missed deadlines: set follow-up reminders for all users.
Get a handle on overtime vs downtime.
  • Easily monitor machine loading and staff activity.
  • Keep cost centres loaded with work.
  • Planning board will ensure jobs are delivered on time.
  • Reminders drive the job forward.
  • Your team is always busy and your machines are productive.
Accounting that is easy to use and crystal clear.
  • No jobs slip through the cracks.
  • Growth as a percentage is automatically shown in the income statement.
  • Year on year changes in net profit, cost of sales and expenses are shown in graph format.
  • Compare yearly changes in assets, liabilities and equity with ease.
  • Records are automatically updated by transactions — no duplicate entries in different formats.
  • Work with one platform — no more systems that don't communicate with each other.
  • Group, filter and drill-down with ease.
  • Import directly from any bank.
Instant reports.
  • Instant access to staff information, customer records, supplier history and production data.
  • View summaries of work done.
  • Easy to use pivot tables. Easily export data.
  • Highly efficient drill-down functionality from the report to live data.
  • Integrated across the whole business — instantly access every part of your business on one platform.

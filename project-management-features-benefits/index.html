Project Management

BOS Project Management Features and Benefits

A robust and organised system for budgeting, planning, executing and tracking projects across an organisation. Whether it's a small internal task or a complex multi-phase project, this tool helps teams stay aligned, control budgets, and meet deadlines.

Discover how these cutting-edge solutions can transform your organisation.

Plan, Execute and Deliver

Structured Project Setup

Create new projects quickly using templates, set budgets, define timelines and assign teams and responsibilities upfront.

Detailed Task & Activity Management

Break projects down into tasks and sub-tasks. Assign team members, set deadlines, and use checklists to track task progress.

Milestone & Timeline Management

Set major project milestones and deadlines to monitor progress against key delivery dates.

Resource Allocation & Tracking

Assign resources based on availability and track actual time spent on tasks, helping you optimise efficiency and manage costs.

Real-Time Progress Monitoring

Access project dashboards and Gantt chart views that provide instant insights into project status, bottlenecks and completion forecasts.

Financial Control and Cost Tracking

Project Costing & Budget Management

Estimate, monitor and control project costs with real-time visibility into budgets, expenditures and variance analysis.

Link to Accounting Modules

Seamlessly connect projects to financial records, ensuring accurate billing, cost recovery and profitability tracking.

Revenue Recognition & Billing

Manage invoicing against project milestones or progress, ensuring accurate and timely billing.

Enhanced Collaboration and Communication

Centralised Project Information

Keep all project-related documents, communications and task updates in one place for better team coordination.

Integrated Checklists & Approvals

Ensure that each project stage or task meets defined quality standards with built-in checklists and approval workflows.

Cross-Department Visibility

Enable collaboration between sales, production and service teams with a shared view of project tasks and responsibilities.

Reporting and Analytics

Interactive Reports

Drill down into project data by task, team, milestone or financials to identify risks, opportunities and performance trends.

Customisable Outputs

Create tailored reports to suit different stakeholders — whether project managers, executives or finance teams.

Forecasting Tools

Predict project completion dates, budget overruns, and resource needs before they become critical issues.

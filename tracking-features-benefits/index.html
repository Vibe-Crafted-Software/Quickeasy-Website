BOS Tracking Features and Benefits

The Tracker App offers a comprehensive solution for real-time tracking of production, timekeeping, inventory, field service activities and digital deliveries. Designed for both shop-floor environments and mobile field operations, Tracker enables organisations to capture critical operational data easily — improving visibility, accuracy, and efficiency across teams.

Shop-Floor Tracking and Timekeeping

Real-Time Job Tracking

Capture the start, pause, and completion of manufacturing or production tasks directly on the shop floor using touchscreen devices or scanners.

Production Board Timekeeping

Track operator activities through a visual interface, providing real-time insights into work-in-progress (WIP), downtime, and operator efficiency.

Multi-Job and Multi-User Handling

Support multiple users clocking on and off various jobs, enabling detailed tracking of time and output per employee or team.

Automatic Status Updates

Update job statuses automatically as tasks progress, ensuring accurate job cards and production records without manual intervention.

Touchscreen-Friendly Interface

Designed for fast and simple operation in manufacturing environments, even for users with limited technical skills.

Inventory Movements

Issue and Receive Inventory

Easily record inventory movements — such as issuing materials to production or receiving finished goods — using barcode scanning or touchscreen interfaces.

Batch and Serial Number Tracking

Capture critical inventory details including batch numbers, serial numbers, and lot tracking for full traceability.

Inventory Reconciliation

Streamline stock adjustments and reconciliation processes by capturing real-time transactions from the floor or field.

Digital Deliveries

Goods Dispatch and Delivery Management

Capture goods issues and delivery transactions digitally when products leave your warehouse or are handed over to customers.

Proof of Delivery (POD) Capture

Record customer signatures or take photos at the point of delivery for immediate, verifiable proof of delivery.

Real-Time Delivery Updates

Track outstanding, completed, pending, or failed deliveries instantly, improving dispatch team coordination.

Time and Attendance

Employee Clock-In/Clock-Out

Allow employees to clock in and out via touchscreen, scanner, or mobile device.

Shift and Break Tracking

Record working hours, overtime, breaks, and shift changes accurately for payroll integration.

Time Validation and Approvals

Supervisors can review, validate, and approve timesheets to ensure payroll accuracy and compliance.

Reporting and Analytics

Real-Time Operational Dashboards

Monitor job statuses, field service activities, inventory movements, and timekeeping from dynamic dashboards.

Detailed Historical Records

Access comprehensive history logs for audits, performance reviews, or customer reporting.

Integrated Analytics

Leverage real-time data for production efficiency analysis, resource planning, inventory control, costs and service quality management.

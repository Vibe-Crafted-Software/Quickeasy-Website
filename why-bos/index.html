Intuitive and user-friendly

Designed around user-friendliness and UX — user-based fields, customisable home screens, fast and easy access to data from anywhere in the system, plus graphical workflow tools and dashboards.

Features galore

Fully stocked with modules, functionality and features to control and manage your whole business, from the cashier and finance to the shop floor and warehouse, across one or many offices, warehouses and currencies.

Lower cost of ownership

An alternative to rigid, expensive and complex traditional ERP solutions — total cost of ownership is dramatically lower, because the system is easier to use, maintain and upgrade.

Beyond innovation

Constantly evolving to deliver the tools and functionality that meet your changing needs. Our development team takes customer feedback seriously and breaks new ground with every version release.

Astonishingly flexible

BOS — particularly BOS Enterprise — is built to mould to your business model, not the other way around. Highly flexible and modular, it adapts to streamline operations and improve efficiency.

Scalable

Perfect for growing companies — a highly flexible, scalable solution that handles an increasing amount of work and accommodates gradual, steady growth as you scale.

Reference customers

An extensive customer register across every vertical we support — Manufacturing, Engineering, Signage, Printing, Packaging, Food and Beverage, Hotels and Construction — serving SMEs locally and worldwide.

On-premises or cloud

Available as an on-premise installation or fully cloud-based, both with 100% functionality. Secure, risk-free and fully protected, BOS Cloud handles the infrastructure so you can focus on your core business.

Empowering the user

BOS empowers users to work independently — at home or in a remote office — creating their own workflows, reminders, schedules and processes as the pace of business evolves.
